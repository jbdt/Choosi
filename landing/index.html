Free Progressive Web App

Chosi Choosi

Multi-touch random finger picker

Everyone puts a finger down. One tap picks winners, splits teams, or sets the order.

Choosi needs a touchscreen, open this page on your phone or tablet to use it.

Three ways to decide

Pick

Everyone puts a finger on the screen at the same time. Choosi randomly picks one, or more if you need multiple winners.

Perfect for settling who pays, who goes first, or who gets the last slice.

Groups

Put all your fingers down and Choosi instantly splits them into equal teams.

No arguments, no bias. Great for team games, secret assignments, or anything that needs a fair split.

Order

Everyone touches the screen and Choosi assigns a random turn order: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, all the way down.

Ideal for board games, presentations, or any time you need to decide who goes when.

Install the app

Runs like a native app — no App Store needed.

AndroidChrome
  1. Open choosi.javier.vn in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right)
  3. Tap "Install and create shortcut"
  4. Tap "Install"
iPhone / iPadSafari
  1. Open choosi.javier.vn in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom
  3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap "Add", it installs as a full app, not a shortcut
